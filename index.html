Cook with me

A little help in your kitchen.

Save your recipes, plan your meals and cook at your own pace. Find help with Tableleaf below.

Recipe imports

A recipe link cannot be read

Some websites and social posts do not expose a complete recipe, or require sign-in. Try the original recipe website, paste the recipe caption, choose a screenshot, or import a video saved to your device. Direct YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Pinterest links are not guaranteed to work.

A recipe photo is missing

Photos are imported when a readable source image is available. Some publishers block image access or provide temporary links. You can edit the recipe and add a photo yourself.

iCloud and shared cookbooks

My recipe is not on my other device

Use the same Apple ID on both devices, enable iCloud Drive and Tableleaf in iCloud settings, and check available iCloud storage. Connect both devices to the internet and open Tableleaf. The first sync can take time. Back up important recipes before troubleshooting; do not delete the app to fix a sync delay.

Sharing with another person

Use a cookbook invitation to share selected recipes with someone using another Apple ID. Your private library is separate. Invitation acceptance, internet access and iCloud availability are required.

Cooking assistance

Using voice and local AI

Open a recipe and choose Cook with me. Tap Speak to start listening, then Finish listening, review your words and tap Ask. Microphone and speech permission are required. Supported speech recognition and Apple Intelligence depend on your device, language and settings. You can type questions and use cooking controls when those features are unavailable.

The assistant can make mistakes. Check cooking temperatures, allergens and food safety against reliable guidance. Nutrition values are estimates, not medical advice.

Get in touch

Open a support request on GitHub for a bug, question or feature request. Include your app version, device model and steps to reproduce the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include passwords, Apple ID details, private recipes, health information or other personal data. Screenshots should have private information removed.
