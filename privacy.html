Privacy policy

Effective November 24, 2026. This policy covers Tableleaf by Red Clay AI, Inc., including its recipe app, extensions and this support website.

Your recipes and local storage

Tableleaf stores recipes, images, collections, meal plans and grocery information on your device. Its widget and extensions use shared app storage to provide their features. Tableleaf does not require a separate account and does not operate a developer-hosted recipe or AI service.

iCloud synchronization and family sharing

When iCloud is available and enabled, supported app data is synchronized through Apple CloudKit using your Apple ID. Your personal library uses your private iCloud database; it is not a public recipe feed. Apple provides the cloud infrastructure under its own privacy policy and terms.

When you choose to share a cookbook, selected content is made available to the people you invite through iCloud sharing. Other participants may retain copies of content they received. Review the sharing participants and permissions before sharing private recipes or photos.

Photos, videos and recipe websites

Tableleaf accesses media you select or share with the app to import recipes and images. Importing a link, downloading a recipe image, or opening a page connects your device to the relevant publisher or hosting service. Those services receive normal network information, such as your IP address and the requested URL, and may apply their own cookies, sign-in systems and privacy practices. The in-app browser uses Apple's WebKit. Tableleaf does not control third-party websites.

Local AI and voice

Supported Apple Foundation Models features run on your device. Tableleaf does not send your prompts to an OpenAI, Gemini, Claude or developer-operated AI server. Availability depends on compatible hardware, language and device settings.

Voice input starts when you tap Speak and grant microphone and speech permission. Tableleaf requires on-device speech recognition and offers typed input when it is unavailable. Listening stops when you finish, leave the assistant, or reach the listening limit. The app does not save an audio recording. Assistant transcripts and conversation are kept for the current session and are not added to your synchronized recipe library. Speech replies use Apple's system speech services.

Advertising, analytics and tracking

Tableleaf contains no advertising or analytics SDKs and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. Apple may process App Store, purchase, diagnostic and iCloud information according to your Apple settings and Apple's policies. Third-party recipe websites follow their own policies.

Your choices and deletion

You can edit or delete content in Tableleaf, manage cookbook sharing, and change iCloud, microphone and speech permissions in system settings. Deletions synchronize when iCloud is enabled and connected; synchronization and Apple's backup retention can affect timing. Removing the app alone does not necessarily remove content stored in iCloud or copies held by sharing recipients. Export important recipes before deleting data.

Support and this website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages and includes no analytics scripts or advertising. GitHub processes normal hosting information under its own privacy statement. If you open a GitHub support issue, the content is public and subject to GitHub's policies. Include only the information necessary to explain the issue.

Children

Tableleaf is a general-audience cooking tool and is not designed specifically for children. We do not operate a system for collecting children's profiles or advertising identifiers.

Questions and changes

For general privacy questions, contact us through the support tracker. Do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue. This policy will be updated here when the app's practices change.
